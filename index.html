New on desktop · Masters › Barcode / QR labels

Design a label once. Print a whole purchase.

A drag-and-drop label designer with a live preview, and a print manager that knows your purchase bills, batches and items.

  • Barcodes and QR. Code 128, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, Code 39, ITF-14 and QR, made inside the app and checked by scanning.
  • Everything movable. Text, merge fields, barcode, QR, logo, lines and boxes. Move, resize, turn 0/90/180/270°, fonts and colours.
  • Merge fields. Item, MRP, price, HSN, batch, mfg and expiry dates, purchase voucher, company and your own UDF values from Tally or Excel.
  • Bulk print. From a purchase voucher (quantity per item, split by batch), from a stock item, or from a filtered item list.
  • Exact size. Thermal rolls or A4 sheets (65-up and more), 7 ready templates, a default design, and true-mm preview or PDF.
  • Works offline. No internet needed to design or print.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I still need Tally?

Yes. The solution works with your existing Tally. The desktop app connects to Tally on your PC and keeps the data in sync.

Can I try it before signing in?

Yes. In the Android app, tap "Try demo (no sign-in)" to explore every report with a sample company, offline.

Do the phone reports match Tally?

The phone works out each report with the same rules as the desktop, and the figures are checked against the desktop on test books.

How many salesmen can I add?

Each plan comes with a salesman limit. Contact us to raise it. A salesman signs in with their own Gmail and sees only what you assigned.

Which label printers work?

Any printer Windows can print to: thermal roll label printers and normal printers with A4 label sheets. Set the paper size in the printer and keep the scale at 100%.

Is it a Tally product?

No. HM Tally Bot is an independent product by HS Software Solutions and is not affiliated with Tally Solutions.

How do I get updates?

Android updates come through Google Play. The desktop app finds new versions itself, downloads them, and restarts when Tally work is done.
