WhatsApp

Your accounts desk, inside every chat.

Your party types one word and gets a rich reply with the PDF. You tap a floating icon over any WhatsApp chat to share, or to make an entry on the spot.

  • Auto-reply bot. Statement, outstanding, bills, receipts, confirmation, items and orders.
  • Fast entry from the bubble. Receipt, payment, sales bill or any voucher, with the chat's party already picked.
  • Share right after Accept. Confirmation message, bill PDF or statement goes into the same chat.
  • Orders on WhatsApp. Numbered menu or a mobile order form. You approve, and the party gets a confirmation PDF.
  • Auto-share new bills. Pick voucher types, and each new bill goes to its party once.
